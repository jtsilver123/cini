## Description
Rating a movie 7/10 means nothing. Knowing you liked it more than
Whiplash but less than Interstellar means everything.

Cini replaces star ratings with quick head-to-head comparisons. Log what
you watched, tap how you felt, and answer a few "which did you like
more?" questions — your new movie lands exactly where it belongs in your
perfectly ordered list, with a score out of 10 that comes from your own
taste.

THE MORE YOU RANK, THE SMARTER IT GETS
• Rec Scores predict how much YOU'll like movies you haven't seen
• Your Want to Watch list sorts itself by what you'll love most
• A taste profile that's actually yours — not an average of strangers

BUILT FOR MOVIE NIGHTS WITH FRIENDS
• See what friends scored a movie before you commit your Friday night
• Send recommendations with a note — they land right in their list
• Hearts, comments, and spoiler-safe notes on every ranking
• Race the leaderboard on films watched, influence, and reviews

EVERYTHING A FILM DIARY SHOULD HAVE
• Import your entire Letterboxd or IMDb history in one drag-and-drop —
  watched films, ratings, watchlist, and custom lists
• A diary of every watch and rewatch: when, where, and who with
• Custom lists you can share
• Where to stream tonight, or real theater showtimes nearby — IMAX,
  Dolby, and bargain screenings flagged
• Export your data anytime as Letterboxd-compatible CSVs

No ads. No selling your data. Just your taste, in order.

Movie data from TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed
or certified by TMDB.
