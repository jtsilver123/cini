Questions

Is Cini free?

Yes — Cini is free on the App Store for iPhone. Ranking, recs, lists, friends, and the web pages are all included.

How is Cini different from star ratings?

Instead of asking “how many stars?”, Cini asks “which did you like more?” Quick head-to-head comparisons build a true ranked order, and each title’s score out of 10 comes from where it lands in your list.

Can I import from Letterboxd or IMDb?

Yes. Export your data from Letterboxd or IMDb and drag it into Cini — your watched films, ratings, watchlist, and custom lists come across.

Does Cini cover TV shows too?

Movies and TV are equal first-class citizens — rank, track, and get recommendations for both.
