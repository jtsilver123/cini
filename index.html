How it works

Rating a movie 7/10 means nothing. Knowing you liked it more than Whiplash but less than Interstellar means everything.

Log what you watched

Search any movie or show, tap how you felt — loved it, fine, or didn’t — and add the details: who you watched with, where, and what you thought.

Compare, don’t rate

Cini shows you two movies you’ve seen and asks which you liked more. A few quick taps slot the new one exactly where it belongs.

Your taste, scored

Every film gets a score out of 10 derived from its place in your order — and Cini predicts how much you’ll like what you haven’t seen yet.
