Send your history to your phone

  1. Choose where you're importing from — Letterboxd, IMDb, Netflix, or a mix.
  2. Export your history — a .zip or a .csv, both work:
    Letterboxd
    Go to letterboxd.com/settings/data → click Export your data (saves a .zip).
    IMDb
    Go to imdb.com → Your Ratings → the ⋮ menu → Export (saves a .csv).
    Netflix
    Go to netflix.com/account/profiles → pick your profile → Privacy and Data Settings → Viewing Activity → Download all (saves a .csv).
  3. Enter the 6-letter code from the Cini app.
  4. Drop your file(s) below — importing from two places? Drop both at once.
